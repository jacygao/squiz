## Intent

- With `claude --plugin-dir ./`, every worktree under `.claude/worktrees/` is inside the loaded plugin, and auto mode refuses a subagent's `Write` there. Loading squiz from a checkout outside the repository fixes it.
- Closes #272

## What changed

- `executing-milestones` § 8 says to load squiz from `../squiz-plugin` and gives the three commands: make the checkout once, start the session from it, refresh it after each merge.
- § 8's "Merged is not loaded" now says a merge reaches a session through the refreshed checkout. A refreshed `src/` or `bin/` reaches the next round without a restart, because each firing of the hook runs `bin/squiz` as a new process. A change to `hooks/hooks.json` waits for a restart.
- A new note, `docs/notes/a-subagent-cannot-write-inside-a-loaded-plugin.md`, records #272's measurement table and the decision.

## What did not change

- How a marketplace install works. An installed plugin's directory is not the project, so #272 does not reach it.
- `docs/specs/milestones.md` names `claude --plugin-dir ./` in the M0 and M1 acceptance criteria. Those record how those milestones were confirmed, not how to dogfood, so they are left alone.
- Older notes that ran with `--plugin-dir ./` are records of those runs and are left alone.
