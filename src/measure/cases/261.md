## Intent

- `docs/notes/an-empty-group-loses-its-number-inside-one-round.md` said an ordinary `sleep` cannot hold a recorded group, because it dies on the round's own `SIGTERM`. Since #247 the reading before `SIGKILL` rests on the pids the first reading found, so the `sleep` is the answer.
- The wrong claim pointed readers at a holder that ignores `SIGTERM`, which was tried and abandoned because it raced the round's own signal.

Closes #258

## What changed

- **Decisions:**
  - The holder is an ordinary `sleep`.
  - Before `SIGKILL`, a group is accepted only where it still holds a pid the first reading found, because a group still holding one never emptied.
  - A group with no holder is refused, which covers a shell without `exec -a`.
- **Needs your input** now says "Nothing". This matches the shell-prefix note.
- **The age-test decision no longer says it contradicts § 4.** § 4 now identifies the group by the holder and by pid, and keeps age only as a refusal.
- **Reference** gains a table of what each reading requires, and the real prefix lines from `groups.ts`.
- **The `before TERM` / `after TERM` output** now shows why the second reading cannot ask for the holder.
- **The `SIGTERM`-and-fresh-descendant gap is named.** A tool that answers `SIGTERM` by starting a new process and exiting is refused at the second reading, and the new process runs on. The abandoned design would have killed it.
- **Limits gains three entries:**
  - The reading by pid also rests on no pid coming round inside the 2-second grace. If the group's shell was alive at the first reading, its pid is the group's number. Should the group empty and that number come to lead a stranger's group within the grace, the second reading would accept it.
  - The holder's 900 seconds were chosen, not measured.
  - A refusal reaches no channel yet (#249).
- **The forged-record result now says it was not re-run** against the holder.

## What did not change

- Every measurement stands: the turnover table, the wrap arithmetic, the `ps -E` table and its sizes, and the forged-record result.
- The figure of 106 seconds stays: the time this measurement took to reach a process of the user's own that the round never started. It sat in the old Needs your input, was dropped in a first draft, and now sits in the first Decision.
- No code or spec changed.
